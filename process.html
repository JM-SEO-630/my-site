FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our shipping process and services.

How long does sea freight from Asia to Mexico take?
FCL transit time is typically 25–35 days for major Korean and Chinese ports to Manzanillo or Veracruz. LCL consolidations add 5–7 days for stuffing and CFS processing. Transit times can vary by carrier and routing — we will confirm the exact schedule at quotation stage.
What documents are required for importing to Mexico?
Core documents include: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading (sea) or AWB (air), Certificate of Origin (if preferential tariff applies), and any NOM/NMX compliance certificates for regulated products. Our customs team prepares and reviews everything before departure.
Do you handle dangerous goods (DG / Hazmat) shipments?
Yes — for air freight, we are IATA-certified for DG handling. For sea freight, we manage IMO-classified dangerous goods subject to carrier acceptance and applicable regulations. Please declare all DG items at consultation stage so we can plan the correct documentation and routing.
Can you deliver to locations outside Mexico City?
Absolutely. Our inland distribution network covers all of Mexico — including major industrial hubs such as Monterrey, Guadalajara, Saltillo, Tijuana, and the Northern Border maquiladora corridors. Both FTL and LTL options are available.
How do I get a quote?
You can request a quote through our Contact page, or email us directly at nosea.op@noseaglobal.com.

For a faster and more accurate quote, please include the following details:
  • Commodity
  • HS Code (if available)
  • Weight / Volume
  • Origin
  • Destination
  • Preferred Shipping Schedule
Once we've reviewed your request, we will send a detailed quote covering freight, customs, and related fees within 48 hours.
